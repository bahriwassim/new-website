RC-5000

Auto Ref-Keratometer

Auto-refractor
Keratometer
central K-values
peripheral K-values

The RC-5000 is a very reliable auto ref-keratometer. Thanks to the electrically controlled measurement head, you can operate and align the RC-5000 with the power-motion joystick and/or touch screen – all in a fraction of a second. Highly accurate measurements combined with the very short examination time and easy handling makes working with the TOMEY RC-5000 fast and professional.

Excellent features

  • Central and peripheral K-values
  • Colour touch screen
  • Pupil and cornea ø measurement
  • Auto alignment and auto measurement
  • Power motion joystick
